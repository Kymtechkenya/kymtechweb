Why Ex-UK, and not just any "cheap" laptop?

Ex-UK units come from corporate and institutional lease returns in the United Kingdom — business-grade machines like ThinkPads, EliteBooks and Latitudes, built for daily office use over 3–4 years. They're a different category from generic low-end laptops sold as "new" but built with budget parts.

Ask us anything about grading & warranty

Built for the enterprise

Business lines (ThinkPad, EliteBook, Latitude) use magnesium-alloy chassis and higher-spec components than most consumer "new" laptops at the same price point.

Graded before sale

Every unit is inspected for battery health, screen condition, keyboard and ports, then labelled Grade A, A- or B so you know exactly what you're buying.

Real cost savings

You get a Core i5/i7 business laptop for roughly the price of an entry-level new machine with a fraction of the processing power.

Backed by warranty

Refurbished doesn't mean unsupported — every Ex-UK unit ships with a warranty period and our own diagnostics report.
